The same three-step process runs on every single project — no exceptions, no shortcuts. Most copywriters write first and research later. I never do. The research is the work. The writing is just the output of understanding your buyer more deeply than they understand themselves.

01 — Before Writing

Understand

Your offer, positioning, brand voice, market, and competitors — so the message has a strategic foundation.

  • Offer & program structure

The people behind the purchase — not just the purchase itself. Their exact language, their real fears, the objections they won't say out loud.
